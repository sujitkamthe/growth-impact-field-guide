# Building Sahaj together: storyboard

The second film, about the Org & Community area: the contributions people make beyond their own project. It plays in the same valley, with the same explorer, villagers, fog of war and game HUD as the guide's film (see `../../STORYBOARD.md` and `../../WORLD.md`), so it feels like another episode of the same game. About two minutes. The narration is in `script.js` beside this file; this file says what is on screen.

## Look and feel

Everything from the first film applies: a high three-quarter game camera, real walking, calm eased motion, the HUD (quest box, toasts, minimap), captions, and Sahaj indigo on the explorer and the HUD. Org & Community's colour in the guide is amber, so this film leans on amber: banners in the square, lanterns, the DevDay festival. The explorer starts at Extended reach, as at the end of the first film's middle chapters (the fog lifted to the Extended ring), and the film may lift it further in "How far it reaches".

Ideas are places and actions in the village, never diagrams:

- **Culture and organisational health**: the explorer helping a new villager settle in (walking them in from the village gate to the houses), and fixing something that wears people down (see the quiet fix below).
- **Initiatives and capability**: a workshop where the explorer and two villagers build a tool at a workbench; an interview at a table in the hall; a newcomer being shown round (onboarding); a capability track as a small class of villagers gathered round one who teaches.
- **Community and external contribution**: DevDay as a village festival in the square: amber banners, lanterns, a small stage with someone speaking to a crowd; a session by the fire; a written note pinned on the notice board for everyone (writing).
- **The quiet fix**: a broken plank on the bridge that every villager stumbles over each day. The explorer quietly fixes it; afterwards the villagers cross smoothly, many times, through the season. It matters more than the festival stage, and the film shows it with the same weight.

## Chapters

### 1. Built by all of us (`together`)
1. Morning over the village. No guild hall, no "culture department": the camera glides over villagers going about their day.
2. The explorer walks into the square, where villagers are each doing something for the village beyond their own fields: one repairs a fence, one teaches two others, one hangs a banner. Quest box: "Contribute to the village".
3. A teammate on the explorer's team gestures towards the square, making room (they take over the explorer's crop work for an hour); the explorer goes. The HUD shows a small clock icon or the sun still high: this is working time, not evenings.

### 2. Three ways to contribute (`ways`)
1. Three signposts or banners in the square, one per way to contribute, in amber; the toast "Three ways to contribute"; quest box "Choose one or more · 0/3".
2. Culture and organisational health: the explorer walks a new villager in from the village edge to the houses and introduces them around (wave); then raises a hand at a small gathering by the notice board (speaking up). Toast "Culture and organisational health"; quest 1/3.
3. Initiatives and capability: the workshop: the explorer and two villagers at a workbench building a tool (Interact), then a small class gathered round a villager teaching (capability track). Toast "Initiatives and capability"; quest 2/3.
4. Community and external contribution: the square turns into DevDay: amber banners unfurl, lanterns light, villagers gather before a small stage where a villager speaks; a note goes up on the notice board (writing). Toast "Community and external contribution"; quest 3/3.

### 3. How far it reaches (`reach`)
Reuse the first film's lavender scope rings (from `chapters/_shared.js`), centred as there.
1. The camera rises; the rings draw on.
2. Direct: inside the Direct ring, the explorer helps one newcomer settle and gives a short session by the fire to a few villagers. Toast "Direct".
3. Extended: the explorer runs a regular gathering (a community of practice) that people from neighbouring plots walk in to join, and carries a DevDay from start to finish (banners up, speakers, villagers following up afterwards). Toast "Extended".
4. Team: the explorer builds something the village needs (a new tool shed with a sign, or a training yard), then hands the key to two villagers, who keep it running while the explorer walks away. Toast "Team".
5. Wider: the camera lifts across the river: people in the hamlet beyond run the same kind of programme, copied from the village. Toast "Wider". The Wider ring glows; then the rings settle.

### 4. What counts (`counts`)
1. The DevDay stage (bright, crowded) on one side and the bridge on the other. Toast "What changed, not how visible".
2. The quiet fix: a villager stumbles on the broken bridge plank; the explorer kneels and fixes it (Interact); then many villagers cross smoothly, quickly, again and again (time-lapse feel, the sun moving).
3. The season passes (sun sweep): the fixed bridge still in use, the tool shed still running with its new keepers. Quest box "A contribution you keep up".

### 5. When there is no room (`room`)
1. The explorer is busy at their crop field from dawn, the square's activity out of reach; they walk to a senior villager and talk (Interact); the HUD shows a small note "Raised early".
2. The team re-arranges: a teammate takes part of the field work, and the explorer walks to the square to contribute, in daylight. Quest box "Room made together".

### 6. Your way in (`close`)
1. Pull up over the lively village square at golden hour: the festival, the workshop, the fixed bridge, villagers everywhere; the area's question as the last toast: "Is Sahaj stronger because you're here?" End card from the player.
