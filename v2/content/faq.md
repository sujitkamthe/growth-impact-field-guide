## Scopes

### Is this a checklist?

No. The statements describe the shape of impact at each scope, and nobody is expected to tick every line. Four statements per area is a deliberate limit. The question in a team check is whether we would trust you with a problem of this scope, not whether you did each item.

### Is my scope a title?

No. It describes the responsibility and impact expected of you, not who you are. "My reference scope is Team" is a fair way to say it; "I'm a Team" isn't.

### What's the difference between Extended and Team?

At **Extended** you improve work beyond your own: a stream you lead, a design others build on, a teammate you helped succeed. At **Team** you improve the system that produces the work: the architecture, the design bar, how people's judgment develops and how the team performs. The test: if you stopped touching individual stories tomorrow, would the team keep working differently because of what you put in place?

### Is leading an account always Wider scope?

No, because an account is not a consistent unit of size. Leading or bootstrapping a one-team account is usually Team scope. Leading a multi-team account, or shaping the client's engineering organisation, is Wider. The scope follows the reach of your impact, not the name of the responsibility.

### Do I need to work across several accounts, or speak at conferences?

No. Deep influence inside one client's organisation, or a capability many Sahaj teams use, counts as much as breadth across accounts. External work is one route to wide impact, never a requirement for it.

### I'm new to Sahaj. Where do I start?

Direct is the baseline for everyone, including experienced hires. You may operate below it for a few weeks in unfamiliar areas, such as a new stack, a new domain or our ways of working. That is ramp-up, not a growth stage, and your expectations should be set at the scope your experience supports once it is over.

## Growth and pay

### How do I move to the next scope?

By sustaining impact at your scope across all three areas, then taking on some of the next scope's responsibilities as a stretch until they are consistent. See [How it works](#how-it-works).

### Do I have to be equally strong in all three areas?

No. Your strength will vary by area, and the rating captures that. What doesn't work is meeting your scope in one area while falling well short in another: that is below expectations in the area that fell short, whatever your strengths elsewhere.

### How does this relate to salary?

Your reference scope is the anchor for compensation: one scope, one band. A stretch doesn't change pay by itself; sustaining it is what makes the case for raising your scope. If you find yourself using the guide to argue for a raise, go back to the evidence first.

### Can I grow without leading a team?

Yes. At Team and Wider scope there are several ways to create impact, including platforms and standards, deep expertise and external work. None is easier, and each still needs impact in People & Team and Org & Community.

## Contribution

### Is Org & Community really mandatory?

Yes. What you contribute is your choice; whether you contribute is not. No meaningful contribution beyond your delivery means Org & Community is below expectations.

### Can I contribute beyond my scope?

Yes, always. Someone at Direct who drives a DevDay or gives a talk is contributing at org level, and that counts in full, often as exceeding expectations. Doing it consistently is also a good sign that it's time to discuss a stretch.

### Does giving a conference talk make me more senior?

Not by itself. Contribution is judged on what changed, not on how visible it was.

### What if my project gives me no room to contribute or grow?

Raise it during the cycle, when it can still be addressed, not at assessment as an explanation. Sahaj creates opportunities, and you are expected to ask for the ones you need.

## The process

### Who sets my expectations?

You and the teammates who see your work, together, at the start of the cycle, written down on an expectation sheet. There is guidance for them in [Running the conversation](#teammates).

### What if my expectations were never set?

That is the first thing to fix, before any self-assessment. Set them now, even mid-cycle, and say that you did.
