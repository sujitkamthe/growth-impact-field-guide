## Account

<!-- matches: account leads, account lead -->
Sahaj's work with one client, whether it has one team or several. The account lead looks after the client relationship and the account as a whole.

## Agreed focus

<!-- matches: agreed focus -->
An agreement, from Extended scope, to take less of a particular responsibility and contribute more through a strength in return. See [Agreed focus](#specialisation).

## Area

One of the three areas of impact that every scope covers: [Client & Delivery](#area/client-delivery), [People & Team](#area/people-team) and [Building Sahaj](#area/org-community). Each area gets one rating.

## Cycle

<!-- matches: cycles, cycle -->
The period one expectations doc covers, usually a quarter. It starts with writing your expectations and ends with the team check.

## Delivery ownership

<!-- matches: delivery ownership -->
The delivery facet of Client & Delivery: taking responsibility for commitments and outcomes, not only finishing your work on time. It includes agreeing what can realistically be delivered, managing the client's expectations, and holding your position, with reasons and an alternative, when a date, scope or pace isn't safe.

## Expectations doc

<!-- matches: expectations docs, expectations doc -->
A short doc you keep for each cycle, made from one template for every scope. It notes your scope, anything specific to your work, any agreed focus or agreed deferral, where you want to grow, and at the end your self-assessment. The expectations themselves stay in the guide. You drive it; your teammates comment on it.

## Facet

<!-- matches: facets, facet -->
One of the three parts of Client & Delivery or of People & Team, such as consulting or growing others. How facets make up one rating is on [Self-assessment](#self-assessment/choosing-a-rating).

## Growing your scope

<!-- matches: someone's scope grows, scope has grown, your scope grows, scope grows -->
Your scope moving to the next one, decided at the team check once your contribution consistently reaches the next scope in all three areas. See [Growing into the next scope](#how-it-works/growing-into-the-next-scope).

## Rating

<!-- matches: ratings, rating -->
How you did in one area at your scope: below, meets or exceeds expectations, judged against your current scope. See [Choosing a rating](#self-assessment/choosing-a-rating).

## Scope

<!-- matches: scopes -->
How far your impact is expected to reach: [Direct](#scope/direct), [Extended](#scope/extended), [Team](#scope/team) or [Wider](#scope/wider). Each wider scope includes the ones inside it. Yours is the one you show consistently across all three areas.

## Seniors

<!-- matches: seniors -->
The person or people on a team who look after its people's growth, and who help settle a disagreement about someone's scope or rating.

## Stretch

<!-- matches: stretches, stretch -->
Some of the next scope's responsibilities, added to your expectations for a cycle. It usually starts in one area.

## Team check

<!-- matches: team checks, team check -->
The conversation at the end of a cycle in which teammates confirm, correct or add to your self-assessment, confirm your ratings, decide whether your scope has grown, and look ahead with you at the next cycle.

## Ways to contribute

<!-- matches: ways to contribute, way to contribute -->
The three groups in Building Sahaj: culture and organisational health, initiatives and capability, and community and external contribution. Unlike facets, you choose one or more rather than showing all three.
