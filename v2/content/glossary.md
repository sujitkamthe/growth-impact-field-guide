## Account

<!-- matches: account leads, account lead -->
Sahaj's work with one client, whether it has one team or several. The account lead looks after the client relationship and the account as a whole, and decides when a disagreement about someone's scope or rating remains after the team's seniors have made a judgement call.

## Area

One of the three areas of impact that every scope covers: [Client & Delivery](#area/client-delivery), [People & Team](#area/people-team) and [Org & Community](#area/org-community). Each area gets one rating.

## Cycle

<!-- matches: cycles, cycle -->
The period one expectations doc covers, usually a quarter. It starts with writing your expectations and ends with the team check.

## Expectations doc

<!-- matches: expectations docs, expectations doc -->
A short doc you keep for each cycle, made from one template for every scope. It notes your scope, anything specific to your work, where you want to grow, and at the end your self-assessment. The expectations themselves stay in the guide. You drive it; your teammates comment on it.

## Facet

<!-- matches: facets, facet -->
One of the three parts of Client & Delivery or of People & Team, such as consulting or growing others. All three count toward the area's one rating: exceeding your scope's expectations in two can carry a weaker third, but a facet not shown at your scope at all makes the area below expectations.

## Raise in scope

<!-- matches: reference scope is raised, raising someone's scope, raise a reference scope, scope should be raised, scope is raised, raise in scope, scope moves up -->
Moving your reference scope to the next one. It is decided at the team check once you show next-scope impact consistently in all three areas, and the people around you depend on it. A stretch is the usual way there but isn't required, and the next cycle's doc is written at the new scope.

## Rating

<!-- matches: ratings, rating -->
How you did in one area at your scope: below, meets or exceeds expectations. Exceeds is judged against your current scope and doesn't change your scope. Your teammates confirm your ratings at the team check.

## Reference scope

<!-- matches: reference scopes, reference scope -->
The scope you show consistently across all three areas, agreed in your expectations doc. It sets the same expectations in every area. The guide also calls it simply your scope.

## Reviewers

<!-- matches: reviewers -->
Anyone on your team who reviews your expectations, gives you feedback or takes part in your team check, whatever their scope.

## Scope

<!-- matches: scopes -->
How far your impact is expected to reach: [Direct](#scope/direct), [Extended](#scope/extended), [Team](#scope/team) or [Wider](#scope/wider). Each wider scope still includes the ones inside it, so your own work keeps counting as your scope grows.

## Seniors

<!-- matches: seniors -->
The person or people on a team who look after its people's growth. When the team can't agree on someone's scope or rating, they make a judgement call.

## Stretch

<!-- matches: stretches, stretch -->
Some of the next scope's responsibilities, added to your expectations for a cycle. It usually starts in one area. Someone who has been at the same scope for a long time has a stretch set in all three areas.

## Team check

<!-- matches: team checks, team check -->
The conversation at the end of a cycle in which teammates confirm, correct or add to your self-assessment and confirm your ratings, and any raise in scope is decided.

## Ways to contribute

<!-- matches: ways to contribute, way to contribute -->
The three groups in Org & Community: culture and organisational health, initiatives and capability, and community and external contribution. Unlike facets, you choose one or more rather than showing all three.
