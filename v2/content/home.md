## How the guide works

Growing at Sahaj means your work does more for clients, for the people around you and for Sahaj. Sahaj has no titles, so the guide describes how far your impact reaches with four **scopes**, from your own work to beyond your team, and your scope tells you and your team what to expect of you. Growing means reaching further rather than climbing a ladder.

## Find your scope

Read the four scopes below and pick the one your recent work has shown consistently, across all three areas. Open it to see what's expected there. If you're new to Sahaj, you agree your first scope with your team from the impact you had in your previous work.

- [Give and ask for feedback](#feedback): Cover all three areas, and look ahead at each team check
- [Write your self-assessment](#self-assessment): Look back on the cycle and rate each area

## Where to go next

## How growth happens

You grow by deepening what you do best, by taking on some of the next scope's responsibilities, or both. Your scope grows once your contribution consistently reaches the next scope in all three areas.
