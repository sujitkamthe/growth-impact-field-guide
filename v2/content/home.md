## How the guide works

Growing at Sahaj means your work does more for clients, for the people around you and for Sahaj: you deepen your expertise, sharpen your judgment, help teammates become more effective, and are trusted with problems that reach further. Sahaj has no titles, so the guide describes that reach with four **scopes**, from your own work to beyond your team. Each includes the scopes inside it, so growing means reaching further rather than climbing a ladder, and your scope tells you and your team what to expect of you.

Every scope expects impact in the same three **areas**: Client & Delivery, People & Team, and Org & Community. Each cycle you agree your expectations with your team, ask for feedback as you work, and look back together on what changed.

## Find your scope

Read the four scopes below and pick the one your recent work has shown consistently, across all three areas. Open it to see what's expected there. If you're new to Sahaj, you agree your first scope with your team from the impact you had in your previous work.

## How growth happens

You grow by deepening what you do best, by taking on some of the next scope's responsibilities, or both. Your scope grows once your contribution consistently reaches the next scope in all three areas.
