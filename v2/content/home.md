## Your scope

Your scope describes how far your impact is expected to reach. Pick one to see what it asks of you; the scope you're held to is the one agreed in your expectations doc.

## Scopes in practice

One piece of client work, handled at each scope. The example is made up, and it follows Client & Delivery only; every scope expects impact in all three areas.

## How growth happens

You grow by taking on some of the next scope's responsibilities until others rely on them. Strong next-scope impact in one area is recognised as exceeding along the way, and your scope is raised once that impact holds in all three areas.
